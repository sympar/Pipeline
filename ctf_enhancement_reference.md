## Dialogue Technique (reusable)
1. **Garbled-speech tokens** encode the mouth/face degrading before the body: `*ggllrk/glurk/splth/glug/gullk*` inserted mid-word — occasional, not every line.
2. **Dominator voice = sadistic calm + relish:** gently offers a choice while making clear there is none ("relax" versus "watch you struggle"). Contrasts sharply with victim panic.
3. **Onlookers (witness lines) widen the humiliation:** framing it as spectacle ("getting this on video", "everyone's gonna see") — public exposure is a core emotional driver.
4. **The resistance→surrender arc** is the CTF emotional spine: "gotta resist" → "I don't care / please" → total blissful submission. Play this beat in the victim's own words.
5. **Physical detail signals the change** character-appropriately: heaviness of balls, stiffening, throbbing, drooling, tingling, "can't sit still."
