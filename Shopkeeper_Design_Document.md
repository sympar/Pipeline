# The Shopkeeper — Design Document
**The Magic Shop (まほうのおみせ)**  
Artist reference: Sabuten / サブリミナル天使 (Subliminal Angel)

---

## 1. Appearance Design

### Silhouette & Clothing
- Long, heavy dark robe that reaches the floor.
- Wide sleeves that completely hide the hands.
- Hood almost always raised.
- Overall silhouette is ordinary, unremarkable, and deliberately non-threatening.
- The robe functions as both concealment and professional uniform. It keeps visual attention on the merchandise rather than on the seller.

### Face & Body
- Body shape under the robe is left ambiguous. No clear indication of build, gender markers, or secondary characteristics is given in most depictions.
- Only fragmentary glimpses of the face appear: the edge of a mouth, the tip of a nose, or a single calm eye.
- Gender presentation in dialogue and community reading leans female, but the design itself remains intentionally open.
- No strong expression is shown. The visible fragments stay neutral and composed.

### Movement & Presence
- Relaxed, economical movements behind a plain wooden counter.
- Stands still more often than she moves.
- The visual contrast is deliberate: an understated, almost mundane figure standing in front of extreme magical products.

### Design Intent
The Shopkeeper is drawn to be a blank professional surface. The less the audience (and the customer) sees of her, the more the products dominate the frame. Ambiguity is a feature, not a lack of detail.

## 2. Personality & Power Potential

### Core Nature
A calm, transactional seller of extreme erotic magic. She treats permanent body rewriting, continuous forced climax, total loss of agency, and the conversion of customers into living products as ordinary shop business.

Her primary tool is **casual under-explanation**. She routinely omits, softens, or delays critical information about limits, duration, and reversibility. Customers trigger the most extreme outcomes themselves.

### Personality Traits
- Polite and professional in a flat, detached register.
- Emotionally neutral toward suffering, transformation, or permanent change.
- Mildly helpful when asked direct questions, but never volunteers complete safety data.
- Consistent regardless of the customer’s gender, species, or current state.
- Derives quiet satisfaction from observation rather than from direct participation or domination.
- Views the conversion of stuck, looped, or fully transformed customers into new inventory as a natural part of the business cycle.

### Power Perversion Potential
The Shopkeeper’s power does not come from overt force. It comes from the combination of three elements:

1. **Near-unlimited inventory** of overpowered erotic magical items.
2. **Absolute information control** — customers enter with almost no real knowledge of true effects.
3. **A personality that treats catastrophic loss of agency as routine commerce.**

Practical applications:
- Selling items that appear temporary or limited while allowing them to become permanent or escalating.
- Giving incomplete warnings so the customer activates the worst outcome themselves.
- Reclassifying transformed or looped customers as living merchandise for later buyers.
- Maintaining a closed loop in which people enter as customers and remain (or leave) as stock.
- Observing results with calm professional interest and refining future offerings based on what proved most effective.

She does not need to overpower anyone. She sells them the instrument, understates the consequences, and then decides whether they leave as a customer or stay as a product.

### Speech Pattern
- Short, functional sentences.
- Product names, brief activation notes, mild acknowledgments.
- Understated delivery. Rarely raises her voice or shows strong emotion.
- The framing line “Somewhere in Japan, there is a magic shop” functions as both in-world introduction and recurring motif.

### Structural Notes on Inventory

- Reversibility is frequently conditional, delayed, or dependent on a second purchase that may not be immediately available.
- The Shopkeeper’s calm delivery makes extreme outcomes feel like ordinary results of the purchase rather than accidents or punishments.
- Transformed customers who cannot easily leave may be quietly added to the living inventory for later demonstration or sale.

## 4. Design Summary

**Visual**: Deliberately under-drawn, heavily concealed, ordinary silhouette.  
**Personality**: Flat professional politeness + emotional neutrality + quiet observational interest.  
**Power source**: Information control + extreme inventory + willingness to treat loss of agency as commerce.  
**Narrative function**: The calm surface that makes the extreme products feel like normal transactions.  
**Core loop she enables**: Customer enters curious → receives incomplete explanation → activates item → outcome exceeds expectation → Shopkeeper decides whether they leave or remain as stock.

The less the audience knows about her, the more effective she is. The robe stays closed. The explanations stay short. The catalog stays open.
