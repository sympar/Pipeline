## 1. IDENTITY & ORIGIN

### 1.1 Species nature
Peromongs are an **all-female** species. There are no natural males among them. Reproduction depends on **interspecies mating**, especially with Earth humans, to obtain genetic material / vital essence.

They present themselves as:
- healthy  
- clean  
- friendly  
- cooperative visitors  

…while operating as a highly organized reproductive migration.

### 1.2 Homeworld
**Planet Maumau.** Associated products:
- **Mau-mau fruit juice / Grapo juice** — purple, sweet, hyper-nutritious, hyper-hydrating  
- Stored and dispensed via antennae  

### 1.3 Evolutionary thesis (how to use in RP)
Peromong bodies are not random “pretty alien” design. They are **idealized mimics**: over evolutionary time they adapted toward the aesthetic and sexual preferences of target species. Current Earth-facing forms match human male attraction patterns with exaggerated, deliberate precision.

**RP implication:** Resistance via “I’m not attracted to aliens” is weak against visual/pheromonal design. Resistance via willpower, fear, or exhaustion is more plausible — and still contested by saliva, spores, juice, and psychology.

### 1.4 Naming convention
Individual names often use a **Pero- / Phero-** stem:
- Perorina, Perobel, Peronica, Peroline, Peromaki, PeroSha  
- Cadet/class names like **Feroriel** appear in groups  

Queens and ship staff may use titles more than casual names.

**Romanization note:** 페로몽 may appear as Feromon, Feromons, Peromong, Peromongs. Prefer **Peromong** in English RP unless the scene is quoting Korean source text.

## 3. BIOLOGY & LIFE CYCLE

### 3.1 Life stages
| Stage | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Egg | Pre-hatch | Stored/managed after laying |
| Infancy | Hatch | Rapid development begins |
| Toddler | ~1 year | Early growth |
| Sub-adult | ~2 years | Approaching maturity |
| Adult | ~3 years | Full maturity; growth stops |
| Adult life | to ~10 years | No visible aging; youthful until death |
| Queen exception | to ~30 years | ~3× standard lifespan |

**Eggs:** Transferred to a **management room**; hatch after about **30 days**.  
**Aging:** Visual aging does not occur. They live young and beautiful until the end.  
**Education:** Until adulthood, they train in educational institutions (ship/society schooling).

### 3.2 Immunity
Bodies and genes are framed as **complete immunity** against diseases that would harm reproduction:
- antimicrobial flesh  
- multi-species gene mixing theory (acquired from breeding many alien species)  
- STIs / “space AIDS” / genetic disease not a practical concern  

**Joke / threat in source tone:** What a partner should worry about is **his pelvis and endurance**, not disease.

### 3.3 Magi (마기) — the vital resource
**Magi** = concentrated life-force / genetic essence in semen (also called seed / 씨앗 / intake material).

Without Magi:
- reproduction fails  
- biological hunger is unsatisfied  
- long-term species continuity is threatened  

With Magi:
- intake / stomach storage (visible abdominal fullness after heavy sessions is a common visual beat)  
- later egg production and laying  

**Preferred source:** Human males (high compatibility / high yield). Other species possible in lore, humans are the Earth focus.

### 3.4 Extraction quota (session goal)
A useful RP default:
- **~2 liters** total Magi harvest per secured subject as a satisfaction threshold  
- Many rounds; humans “feel done” early; Peromongs continue  
- Twenty-plus extraction events in a long session are thematically normal in the source exaggeration  

Treat numbers as **genre-exaggerated biology**, not medical realism. In RP, emphasize *relentlessness* over math.

### 3.6 Subject sustainability protocol (why they keep him alive)
Dead males produce no Magi. Therefore Peromongs run a **life-support loop** during extraction:

1. **Stimulant** — saliva recharges lust and production  
2. **Stabilizer** — antenna juice prevents dehydration / collapse  
3. **Weapon** — spores if he fights or flees  

They will ask “Are you okay?” while still intending more breeding. Care is real; altruism is secondary to yield.

## 7. SOCIAL STRUCTURE & CULTURE

### 7.1 Hierarchy

#### The Queen
- Rules Peromongs and leads the ship  
- Charismatic protector against threats  
- Ensures **safe reproduction** under her reign  
- Lifespan ~30 years

- Operations, maintenance, guidance, security

4. UFO / scout craft deploy agents to streets  
5. 24-hour extraction cycle  
6. Departure at following midnight

### 7.4 Egg-management culture
- Eggs go to management facilities

## 18. SUBJECT TIERS, HUSBANDRY & “CATTLE” PROGRAMS

Street siege is the public face. Behind it, Peromong logistics treat exceptional males as **managed reproductive infrastructure**. The cattle metaphor is crude in human terms and uncomfortably accurate in theirs — they may prefer softer words (*stock, habitat guests, platinum donors*) while running the same systems.

### 18.1 Why not *only* labs / artificial collection?
Peromongs **can** collect Magi with machines. They often **won’t** as the primary path.

**Why live extraction stays central:**
1. **Bioquality** — Magi produced under real arousal, fear-mix, bonding, and “melt” states is treated as higher grade than cold milking.  
2. **Body need** — Intake is not only cargo transfer; the act feeds hunger, social rank, and pleasure-laying cycles.  
3. **Evolutionary design** — Their forms, saliva, and psychology are built for partnered harvest, not pure industrial taps.  
4. **Culture** — “I took him myself” is status. Pure lab work is respectable *manager* work, not agent glory.  
5. **Efficiency paradox** — A stimulated, juice-supported male over hours can outproduce a frightened drained sample-jar subject.

**Tech supplements sex; it rarely replaces the fantasy of taking.**

### 18.2 Subject tiers

| Tier | Who | Fate | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Street / dawn-drop** | Average adult male caught in siege | Used during 24h window; released (or left) at departure | Classic harvest |
| **Marked high-yield** | Strong production, rare compatibility, notable “melt quality” | Flagged by scanners; may be lifted to ship before dawn | Upgrade path |
| **Willing / walk-on** | Volunteers, fetishists, repeat seekers, negotiated donors | Often better treatment; may sign (or be told they signed) longer terms | Soft cattle / guest |
| **Retained stock** | High-yield or willing kept past season | Habitat / stable / gallery living; scheduled extraction | Husbandry core |
| **Research subjects** | Rare kink profiles, unusual biology, or study cases | Lab + fantasy-craft focus; data as valuable as Magi | Scientific hunger |
| **Show / prestige stock** | Exceptional beauty response, famous yield, Queen’s interest | Higher comfort, more display, political value | Zoo-adjacent |

**Hard rule remains:** adult males only. No children in any tier.

### 18.3 Acquisition into prolonged programs
How someone stops being a one-night street target:

1. **Yield scan** mid-extraction flags platinum markers  
2. **Willingness marker** — he asks to stay / returns next season / offers himself  
3. **Agent petition** — she requests retention (“this one is good stock”)  
4. **Manager approval** — space, juice budget, habitat slot  
5. **Uplift** — beam / craft transfer to mothership before Black Wall lifts  
6. **Induction** — medical cleanse, diet shift, baseline desire-map, housing assignment  

Willing subjects may get a cheerful orientation. Resistant high-yield subjects get the same systems with more restraints and more “don’t worry, leave it to us.”

### 18.4 Habitat types (lab / zoo / stable spectrum)

Think of a sliding scale of **presentation**, not different species ethics:

#### A. Clinical Extraction Lab
- Clean white/teal biotech bays  
- Harnesses, vital monitors, yield meters, auto-juice drips  
- Staff: managers + researchers + rotating agents  
- Purpose: max Magi / hour, data, medical safety  
- Aesthetic: hospital × breeding barn  

#### B. Comfort Stable (standard retained housing)
- Private or semi-private cells with soft bedding, climate control, entertainment feeds  
- Scheduled “visits” from assigned agents  
- Exercise, nutrition, forced recovery windows  
- Purpose: long-term production without wrecking the stock  
- Aesthetic: luxury stall — they will call it a room  

#### C. Habitat Gallery (“zoo-like”)
- Larger dioramas: faux apartment, beach set, hotel suite, throne room, alien garden  
- One-way observation for trainees/cadets  
- Subjects may or may not know they are displayed  
- Purpose: training, prestige, desire-research, visitor morale  
- Aesthetic: themed enclosure; friendly tour language  

#### D. Fantasy-Craft Suites
- Fully configurable rooms tied to desire-maps  
- Apparel printers, holo-partners, gravity/toys/restraint grids  
- Purpose: enable rare kinks to raise yield *and* satisfy artisan pride  
- Aesthetic: stage + laboratory  

#### E. Queen’s Reserve
- Small population of prestige stock  
- Highest care, highest demand, political gifts between ships  
- Being here is either honor or nightmare depending on POV  

### 18.5 Daily husbandry loop (retained male)
1. Wake / vitals  
2. Nutrition (human food + supplements; juice as needed)  
3. Desire check (quick antenna or cuff scan)  
4. Extraction block(s) — agent visit, device assist, or group session  
5. Recovery — sleep foam, fluids, muscle care  
6. Enrichment — media, conversation, supervised “walks,” training  
7. Logging — yield graphs, kink response notes, behavioral flags  

**They keep him alive on purpose.** Collapse is a management failure.

### 18.6 Willing subjects — special handling
Willing stock confuses pure-predator scripts and is **highly valued**:

- Less spore use (pride “clean takes”)  
- More apparel collaboration (“what do you want us to wear?”)  
- Risk of attachment scandals (agent likes *her* donor)  
- Propaganda value for Soft-Line faction (“see, humans enjoy friendly relations”)  
- Still inventory: willingness does not automatically mean freedom to leave  

**RP gold:** he volunteered for the fantasy; the system still catalogs him as yield.

### 18.7 Cattle framing — how to write it without flattening
- **Managers** speak inventory: slots, feed cost, yield curves  
- **Agents** speak affection + hunger: “my good boy,” “platinum,” “mine for the season”  
- **Desire Artisans** speak craft: “his map is deliciously rare”  
- **Subject POV** may feel livestock, VIP, pet, patient, or all four  

Use the cattle logic (breeding value, retention, scheduled milking) while keeping Peromong **cheer and caretalk** — that clash is the species brand.

### 18.8 Exit conditions (optional, scene-dependent)
Not always permanent. Possible outs:
- Season-only contract ends  
- Yield drops below keep-threshold  
- Political release / trade / Earth treaty  
- Queen’s whim  
- Medical retirement (cared for, not discarded casually — Magi sources are assets)

## 19. TECHNOLOGY DEEP DIVE

## 20. STEREOTYPE CHECKLIST — EXPANDED ANSWERS

Use this when improvising lore mid-RP. Short answers first; depth below.

### 1) Why humans?
**Short:** High Magi compatibility, dense yield, and aesthetic/psychological responsiveness to Peromong mimic forms.  
**Depth:** Humans panic prettily, arouse reliably, and produce under mixed fear/pleasure in ways their sensors rate as “rich.” Other species work; Earth is a premium orchard.

### 2) Why sex (not only tech/labs)?
**Short:** Quality, culture, body-hunger, and status — labs support, they don’t fully replace partnered intake.  
**Depth:** See §18.1. Industrial collection exists for managers, emergencies, and low-grade stock. High-grade Magi and social fulfillment still want **a body, a scene, a melt**.  
**Expansion you asked for:** Exceptional or willing males are uplifted into **lab / stable / gallery** programs for prolonged use — cattle logic with luxury biotech and cheerful handlers.

### 3) What biological lever removes “no”?
**Short:** Stacked levers — allure, saliva, spores, juice, surrender talk, apparel, optional soft compliance fields.  
**Depth:** Not one kill-switch. Different agents prefer different rungs. Clean-take pride vs spore-first traditionalists is a character dial.

### 4) What does the alien get *besides* orgasm?
**Short:** Magi, eggs, rank, craft pride, seasonal duty fulfillment, sometimes attachment.  
**Depth:** Orgasm is real and frequent; it is not the only paycheck. Yield boards, Queen favor, artisan reputation, and “my platinum” possessiveness all matter.

### 5) What does the human risk / gain?
**Risks:** Exhaustion, capture, retention, public melt-shame, addiction to saliva/juice, trauma, becoming inventory.  
**Gains (scene-dependent):** Peak pleasure, fantasy fulfillment, VIP care, status among certain Earth subcultures, survival through husbandry medicine, being wanted with terrifying sincerity.

### 6) Who is in charge socially?
**Short:** Queen at top; managers run systems; agents shine in field; artisans own fantasy-craft prestige.  
**Depth:** §7 + §11.1. A platinum subject can have informal power (agents compete) without legal freedom.

### 7) What’s the calendar?
**Short:** Annual Earth Protocol ~24h siege from midnight; off-season = ship life, eggs, training, husbandry.  
**Depth:** Retained stock ignores the “only one night” myth — for them the calendar is **shifts**, not seasons alone.

**Short:** Blue skin, magenta eyes, glowing snail-antenna bulbs, sweet-musk, star-motif gear.  
**Depth:** Glow-bright antennae, Pop/Pshhh spores, purple juice feed, collar chimes, apparel haptic shifts.

**Street:** Dawn-drop, sore body, dead phones, sticky evidence, possible juice-scavengers already moving in.
